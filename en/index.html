07Questions

Before you order

All questions
Is cutting free?
Yes. Whatever cutting style you choose (kabsa pieces, cubes, slices, steaks, mince…) costs nothing. The only paid extras are marinating, skewering and vacuum packing — and their prices are shown before you add them.
How does the advisor calculate quantities?
In grams per person depending on the occasion: 350 g for boneless BBQ, 450 g for bone-in kabsa and mandi, 300 g for steak and 250 g for broth — rounded to the nearest half kilo. You can change any number before you order.
Can I change the advisor's plan?
Yes — tap any earlier answer in the chat to change it, or adjust the weights in your cart. The plan stays saved in your account.
